# Challenge site photos

23 photos of the bike parking at the Endress+Hauser office areas, taken on
29 October 2026 between 08:36 and 08:45. Endress+Hauser provided them as part
of the BaselHack 2026 challenge (see
[`documentation/challenge.md`](../../documentation/challenge.md)); they belong
to Endress+Hauser and are used here for the challenge only.

We used them to place the slots in
[`assets/campus-map/slots.json`](../campus-map/slots.json) and as the
`reference_photo` of each shelter layout in `code/scene-generator/layouts/`.
